# Stage 4: Pads and FX

Load with `_common.md`. Goal: harmony and space that support the hook without filling the mids.

## Chords and pads
- Progression from the key: tech house often a single chord or 2-chord loop (i–VI or i–iv); progressive: 4 chords over 16 bars, slow.
- Voicings: rootless 7ths and 9ths in the C3 to C5 range; leave the bass register empty.
- Stabs for tech house (short, rhythmic, swung); wide evolving pads for Prydz-style breaks (slow attack, filter LFO, Valhalla Supermassive).
- High-pass pads at 200 to 300 Hz. If the mid band is over the reference, cut pad level or voicing first.

## Textures and FX
- Atmosphere: noise beds, field recordings, vinyl or room tone at low level for width.
- Risers: white noise with rising filter and volume over 8 or 16 bars, into Supermassive. Downlifters after drops.
- Impacts and crashes on section starts; reverse cymbals one bar before.
- Keep FX on one or two tracks (Lite limit).

## Done when
The full-loop capture's mid band is within about 2 dB of the reference, nothing masks the hook, version saved and rated.
