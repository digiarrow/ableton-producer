# Stage 1: Drums

Load with `_common.md`. Goal: a groove that rolls on its own for 8 bars and matches the reference's drum feel.

## Order
1. **Kick.** Four-on-the-floor. Pick from `sound_palette/kick`, then sample packs. Tech house: short punchy 120 to 200 ms, tuned to the key root (F to A). Progressive techno: longer, rounder tail. Compare the solo kick capture's sub and low bands with the reference drop's first beat.
2. **Clap or snare** on 2 and 4. Layer a clap with a short snare or rim if thin. Tiny pre-delay (5 to 15 ms late) gives swing.
3. **Hats.** Closed 16ths (or 8ths) with velocity accents; open hat on every offbeat ("&"). Short decays.
4. **Shakers and percussion.** Shaker 16ths; congas, bongos, rims, toms or cowbell in 3-3-2 or call-and-response figures over 2 bars. Carl Cox: tom rolls and loops. Carola: few elements, small changes. Don't Blink: tight bouncy rims and snaps.
5. **Groove and swing.** 54 to 58% swing on hats and percussion (Live groove pool, or move 16th offbeats 10 to 20 ms late). Kick and clap stay straight.
6. **Velocity variation.** Hats 70 to 110, accents on offbeats; ghost notes at 40 to 60. Nothing at fixed 127.
7. **Drum bus check.** Kick peaks about -6 dBFS alone; nothing clips; hats not brighter than the reference (compare air band); onset density within about 1 hit/s of the reference.

## Track layout (Lite, 8 tracks)
Kick on its own track (needed for ducking the bass). Clap, hats and rides on one Drum Rack ("Drums Top"). Percussion on one Drum Rack ("Perc").

## Done when
The 8-bar loop's onset density and high-mid/air bands are close to the reference drop, the version is saved and rated, and the checklist's Drums box is ticked.
