# Creative direction

From Digi Arrow's improvement plan (2026-11-27). Read it at the start of every original track, with `taste_log.md` and `session_notes.md`.

- **Main lane: tech house.** Other genres only when Digi Arrow asks or the song is a reference study.
- **Aim to be liked and to sell**, not for a personal signature sound. When a choice is unclear, pick what works on dancefloors and charts now.
- **Follow what is charting and selling.** Read the newest trend report in `/mnt/project-files/scout/` (`<date>-trends.md`). If none is under 4 weeks old, say so and ask for a scout run before the setup step ends. Write in the song sheet which 1 to 3 trend cues the track follows (tempo, drum style, bass type, hook type, arrangement).
- **DJ-friendly arrangement:** every track starts and ends with a **drums-only** intro and outro (16 to 32 bars, clean 8-bar phrases), so DJs can mix it in and out. Bass and music come in after the intro and leave before the outro.
- **Groove first, then one hook.** Build drums, bass and groove before anything else. Then add **one** simple, repetitive, memorable element that gives DJs a crowd reaction point: a short spoken or chopped vocal hook, or a synth stab. One strong hook beats a crowded arrangement; extra parts support it, they don't compete with it.

Exception: in a remake or reference study, Digi Arrow wants **no vocals or vocal-like layers** (2026-11-24). There the hook is a synth stab, lead or pluck, never a vocal chop or choir filling the reference's vocal slot.
